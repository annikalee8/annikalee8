The role

Turning dense sustainability requirements into something people could actually use.

At Azilis Advisory in Copenhagen, I supported project design, marketing development and sustainability-related research. A major part of the work involved translating complex ESG and reporting material into clearer structures, learning experiences and project outputs.

Because the work was covered by an NDA, this public case study deliberately stays at the level of process, skills and outcomes rather than showing internal screens, client information or proprietary platform content.

04

Takeaway

Clarity is a product decision.

The internship strengthened my ability to work with ambiguity, ask better questions and simplify information without flattening the substance. It also gave me experience collaborating in an international startup environment where priorities evolved quickly.
