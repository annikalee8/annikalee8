Energy Club

A student-focused market research project connecting pricing, product awareness and promotion to concrete recommendations.

Findings are from an academic research project; confidential sales and revenue figures were not disclosed by the business.

The question

How could Body Energy Club become more relevant to students beyond smoothies?

The project examined product awareness, willingness to pay and promotions for the UBC location. The team combined survey, focus-group, interview and secondary research, then used statistical testing to compare active and non-active students.

Recommendations

Use the research to change visibility, promotion and customer relationship.

The recommendations moved supplements into higher-traffic areas, used sampling around campus, strengthened loyalty and CRM, and made the brand more approachable through ambassadors and clearer student-facing communication.

Takeaway

Good research changes who you target, what you emphasize and where you show up.

The project connected qualitative insight, competitor analysis and statistical testing to concrete marketing decisions.
