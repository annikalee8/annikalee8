My contribution

Two audiences, two voices, one organized execution system.

I supported two creator accounts with very different audiences: one focused on running, holistic wellness and lifestyle content; the other focused on motherhood, baby products and practical family content.

My role covered content scheduling, campaign ideation, original visual content and video editing, layout planning and ongoing analytics review. The goal was not to force both accounts into one formula; it was to build a repeatable workflow while protecting each account's tone, visual identity and audience expectations.

What I learnedConsistency is a system. Voice is a choice.

Takeaway

Good social strategy adapts to the audience, not the template.

This work strengthened my ability to move between brand voices while keeping calendars, campaigns, creative execution and analytics organized. The result was more consistent output and stronger average engagement without flattening the personality of either account.
