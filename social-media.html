The work

Two audiences, two voices, one repeatable planning process.

I supported an independent social media manager working across two lifestyle-focused accounts: one centered on running, holistic wellness and lifestyle content, and another centered on motherhood, baby products and practical family content.

My work included content schedules, campaign ideas and layout planning. The challenge was keeping the planning system consistent without making the two brands sound or look the same.

Takeaway

Good social strategy adapts to the audience, not the template.

The work strengthened my ability to shift between brand voices while keeping calendars, campaigns and visual planning organized.
