The role

Adapting one planning process to two very different audiences.

I worked alongside an independent social media manager supporting two lifestyle-focused client accounts. One centered on running, lifestyle content and holistic wellness; the other focused on motherhood, baby products, practical tips and family life. My work included building content schedules, developing campaign ideas and planning visual layouts while keeping each account's tone and audience distinct.

Audience 01

Lifestyle, running & holistic wellness.

Content needed to feel personal, motivating and visually cohesive while balancing lifestyle storytelling with wellness-oriented information. Planning focused on maintaining a recognizable tone while creating enough variety to keep the feed useful and engaging.

Audience 02

Motherhood, baby products & practical family content.

This account required a warmer, more practical approach: useful tips, product-oriented content and everyday parenting topics presented in a way that felt approachable rather than overly promotional.

Takeaway
Good social strategy is not one voice applied everywhere. It is a repeatable process that still adapts to the audience.

This experience strengthened my ability to move between brand voices, organize recurring content needs and turn broad themes into practical schedules and campaigns.
