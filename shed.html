SHED
it

A reusable, modular pet-hair removal concept built from observation, opportunity scoring and trade-off analysis.

Academic new-product-development concept; not a commercial launch or tested market result.

Discovery

The research changed the product more than the brainstorming did.

The team studied the everyday pet-hair problem through observation, interviews and outreach across cleaning, veterinary and retail contexts. Rather than assuming “better lint roller” was the answer, we scored opportunities and followed the highest-value unmet needs.

40+professionals contacted across relevant industries
25+individuals interviewed
8.82top opportunity score: emergency removal
68%$10-$40 willingness-to-pay range in survey
Concept

Contain the hair instead of redistributing it.

The final concept became a compact, modular system with multiple cleaning functions and an enclosed collection chamber. The design addressed urgent clothing cleanup, pre-wash removal and hard-to-reach areas while avoiding disposable adhesive sheets.

01

02

Score

Rank needs by importance and satisfaction.

03

Trade off

Remove lower-value features that diluted the concept.

04

Design

Build one reusable system around the highest-scoring jobs.

Takeaway

Ordinary recurring frustrations beat technically interesting distractions.

The project reinforced the value of open-ended research: the strongest opportunities were specific moments of frustration, not the broad category we started with.
