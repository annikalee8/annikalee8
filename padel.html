Padel
It! Vancouver

A market-researched business concept for Vancouver’s first central dedicated indoor padel club.

Academic project. All financial figures are projections rather than operating results.

The opportunity

Vancouver had interest in padel, but not enough accessible indoor infrastructure.

The team explored a dedicated indoor padel concept for central Vancouver. Primary research highlighted travel time, price perception and the seasonal “winter gap” as barriers to participation.

The concept

Accessible, year-round, pay-per-play padel in the urban core.

The proposed model used four indoor courts, central access and no membership requirement. The strategy combined online booking, partnerships, events and community-building to reduce the private-club stigma around the sport.

Takeaway

Research is most useful when it changes the offer.

The strongest part of this project was connecting primary research to product, pricing, location and financial decisions rather than treating research as a standalone slide.
