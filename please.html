Beverage Co.

An integrated digital marketing strategy spanning discoverability, conversion, paid media, social, lifecycle and acquisition economics.

The strategy was developed for a live Vancouver beverage brand, but the recommendations, forecasts and financial scenarios are academic project work rather than claimed implementation results.

Starting point

A strong local product still needs a strong digital path to purchase.

The audit found community momentum and an existing tasting-room experience, but a thin digital acquisition and measurement foundation. Search visibility was concentrated in generic terms, paid search was inactive, and the site had no established SEO, analytics or A/B testing system.

System

Build one connected digital system, not a list of channel tactics.

The sequence connected SEO, CRO, paid search, social, creators, email and retargeting. The goal was for each channel to improve the next one: get found, convert better, create demand, nurture the relationship and recover drop-off.

01

SEO

Improve high-intent discovery.

02

CRO

Test landing pages, CTAs, pricing and social proof.

03

Paid

Capture existing branded, product and event demand.

04

Lifecycle

Use social, email and retargeting to create and retain demand.

Economics

Put an economic guardrail around acquisition.

The team modeled break-even thresholds using average order value, margin, conversion and repeat-purchase assumptions. The point was not to produce a guaranteed forecast; it was to make paid-channel recommendations answer the question of whether acquisition could be profitable.

Academic projectionForecasts are decision tools, not claimed results.

Budgets, CPA thresholds and financial scenarios in this case were planning assumptions developed for coursework.

Takeaway

The strongest channel plan is the one where each part makes the next part work better.

This project reinforced full-funnel thinking: traffic generation, conversion, retention and economics need to be designed together.
