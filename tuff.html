Digital marketing / E-commerce / Growth strategy

A 12-month DTC growth strategy built around fixing conversion and retention before buying more traffic.

Individual university case. Recommendations and projections were developed from the case brief, course frameworks, external research and educated assumptions; they were not implemented client results.

The diagnosis

The case did not point to a traffic problem. It pointed to a conversion and retention problem.

The starting data showed a brand already attracting meaningful attention: roughly 10,000 monthly visitors, a 12% add-to-cart rate and strong email opens. At the same time, the case baseline showed a 1% conversion rate, 20% repeat purchase rate and heavy reliance on paid traffic. I reframed the challenge around three connected systems: Convert, Retain and Acquire.

Audit & journey

Map where the customer journey was leaking before prescribing new channels.

The site audit separated what was already working - brand voice, visual identity, product appeal and existing traffic - from the friction preventing more visitors from converting. The biggest opportunities included missing product reviews, limited cross-selling, no browse-abandonment flow, checkout friction and a fragmented path between Tuff Athletics' Costco recognition and the DTC site.

Strategy system 01

Convert: improve the funnel before scaling acquisition.

The first priority was to get more value from traffic already arriving. The plan combined qualitative diagnosis with Shopify-native fixes: reviews, accelerated checkout, free-shipping progress, a "Complete the Set" cross-sell, and A/B testing around the Maven Studio collection experience.

Strategy system 02

Retain: turn email and loyalty into a lifecycle, not a sequence of one-off campaigns.

The case showed an 8% email capture rate and strong opens, but only a small share of revenue attributed to email. I proposed rebuilding the lifecycle around four flows: welcome, browse abandonment, cart abandonment and post-purchase nurture. A tiered Capsule Club loyalty concept then extended the strategy beyond the second purchase.

01

Welcome

Lead with brand story first, then introduce the offer instead of training new subscribers to expect an immediate discount.

02

Browse abandonment

Reconnect with visitors who viewed products but never added to cart.

03

Cart recovery

Use a multi-email sequence that progresses from value reinforcement to urgency and only then an incentive.

04

Post-purchase

Cross-sell complementary pieces, introduce Maven Studio, request reviews and trigger winback messaging.

Strategy system 03

Acquire: use the brand equity Tuff already owned before buying more attention.

The most differentiated idea in the strategy was the "Costco Heritage Bridge." Tuff Athletics already had years of recognition with Costco shoppers, but that trust was not clearly connected to the DTC site. The proposal used branded search, an SEO landing page, social storytelling and Maven Studio as the next chapter of a brand customers already knew.

CORE CREATIVE INSIGHT From Costco recognition to DTC discovery.

Use the existing origin story as an acquisition asset rather than treating every visitor like cold traffic.

Campaign concepts

Build content and launches around behavior, not just posting cadence.

The channel plan used TikTok for discovery, Pinterest for purchase-intent behavior and micro-influencers for trackable reach. For Maven Studio, I proposed a seasonal "Drop" model with teaser content, waitlist capture, member early access and post-launch retargeting to create a stronger launch rhythm than routine product uploads.

Execution plan

Sequence the work by impact and effort.

The plan was deliberately staged rather than presented as a list of disconnected tactics. The first 90 days begin with diagnosis, then fix the largest leaks, then layer on compounding systems such as lifecycle email, SEO, owned social and launch mechanics.

W1-2

Diagnose

Review sessions, audit search visibility, subscribe to the existing email flow and confirm tracking before changing anything.

W3-4

Fix the biggest leaks

Add reviews, restructure welcome messaging, improve checkout and install the free-shipping progress bar.

M2

Build lifecycle systems

Launch browse abandonment, cross-selling, post-purchase nurture and the Costco SEO bridge.

M2-3

Build owned demand

Launch TikTok and Pinterest, prepare the Maven waitlist and begin a repeatable content cadence.

Financial thinking

Attach the strategy to economics, while keeping projections clearly separate from results.

The case model translated conversion, average order value, email contribution, repeat purchase and traffic-mix assumptions into a Month 12 target state. These numbers were used to test the scale of the opportunity and prioritize tactics; they are modeled academic projections, not actual business performance.

All Tuff performance targets and financial figures shown on this page are academic case projections based on the information provided in the course case and stated assumptions.
Takeaway
Growth strategy is often less about adding more tactics and more about finding the few systems that reinforce one another.

This project pushed me to connect customer journey diagnosis, e-commerce economics, CRM, brand storytelling, organic content and paid media into one operating sequence. The strongest recommendation was not simply a channel choice; it was recognizing that Tuff's existing Costco heritage could reduce acquisition friction across search, social and retention.
