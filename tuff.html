Individual academic caseUBC Sauder · COMM 464 · 2026

Tuff
Collection

A 12-month DTC growth strategy built around fixing conversion and retention before buying more traffic.

Recommendations and projections were developed from the course case, external research and stated assumptions; they were not implemented client results.

Diagnosis

The case did not point to a traffic problem. It pointed to a funnel and channel-mix problem.

The strategy started from a brand already receiving meaningful traffic and add-to-cart activity. The goal was to get more value from the audience already arriving before scaling acquisition.

10Kmonthly visitors in the case baseline
12%add-to-cart rate
1%conversion rate
20%repeat purchase rate
Strategy

Convert first. Retain second. Acquire more efficiently.

The recommendations combined CRO, lifecycle email, loyalty, paid search efficiency, owned organic channels and a differentiated “Costco Heritage Bridge” that connected long-standing Tuff Athletics recognition with the newer DTC business.

01

Convert

Reviews, checkout, cross-sell and A/B testing.

02

Retain

Welcome, browse, cart, post-purchase and loyalty flows.

03

Acquire

Branded search, SEO, social and creator discovery.

04

Sequence

Prioritize by impact and effort across a 12-month roadmap.

Takeaway

Growth strategy is often about connecting systems, not adding tactics.

This individual case pushed me to connect customer journey diagnosis, e-commerce economics, CRM, brand storytelling and media planning into one operating sequence.
