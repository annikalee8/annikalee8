Why gaming

Turning personal interest into sharper category insight.

PC gaming is a long-standing personal interest, so these case studies gave me the chance to approach a familiar industry from a business perspective. I examined major publishers including Activision and Electronic Arts through the lens of marketing, competitive positioning and player expectations.
