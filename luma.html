Opportunity

The market tension was demographic and operational.

The Germany-focused project connected an aging population, healthcare labor shortages and digital-health readiness to a new wearable service concept. Technology was framed as a way to extend access and continuity rather than as novelty.

Concept

Connect the wearable, the physician and the intelligence layer.

LUMA Health combined continuous health tracking with physician connectivity and AI-supported interpretation. The operating strategy also had to account for privacy, regulatory approval, institutional partnerships and technology adoption among older users.
