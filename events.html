EVENTS
IN MOTION.

Planning / Operations / Guest experience

Coordinating the moving parts behind an event while also thinking about the details guests actually see, use and remember.

The work

Making the logistics feel invisible and the experience feel considered.

My independent event work has included organizing timelines, communicating with clients and vendors, supporting setup and operations, and adapting quickly when plans change. I have also created guest-facing materials such as activity sheets and themed collateral, which means I am thinking about both the operational plan and the experience people have once they arrive.

Event timelinesClient communicationVendor coordinationSetup & logisticsGuest-facing collateralOn-site problem solving
What the design work adds

Collateral is part of the event, not an afterthought.

For private events, I have created materials that support both the theme and the flow of the day. The goal is not simply to make something decorative; it is to make instructions clear, activities easy to use and the overall experience feel cohesive.

01

Takeaway
The best event work is both operational and human: the plan has to function, but the details still have to feel thoughtful.

Events have sharpened my attention to detail, calm under pressure and ability to manage people, logistics and presentation at the same time.
